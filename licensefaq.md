
# Marriage License FAQ
[Back to Home](index.md)

Data current as of July 26, 2026, specific to Pinal County in Arizona

## How much does the marriage license cost?

The Pinal County marriage license costs **$98**. This is the only required expense.

## What should we say when applying?

Let the clerk know that an **ordained minister will perform a religious ceremony**. If you are asked to choose between a religious and civil ceremony, select **religious**.

## How early can we get the license?

The marriage license is valid for **up to one year from the date it is issued**, and there is no waiting period. It may be used anywhere in Arizona.

## Who needs to sign it?

Please choose **two witnesses who are at least 18 years old** and who will be present at the ceremony.

On the wedding day, the marriage license will be signed by:

* Both of you
* Both witnesses
* Your officiant

## What happens to the license after the ceremony?

Your officiant is responsible for returning the completed license to Pinal County within **30 days of the wedding**. It will be mailed on the **next business day** so it is submitted well before that deadline.

## Is there a charge for the ceremony?

No. The ceremony, officiant services and any officiant-related costs are our gift to you.

## Will we receive a marriage certificate?

Yes. A signed ceremonial marriage certificate will be provided to you **free of charge** after the ceremony.

This certificate may be accepted by the Social Security Administration as evidence of the marriage when requesting a legal name change and replacement Social Security card. It is separate from the official marriage record later recorded by Pinal County.

## Will we need a certified copy from Pinal County?

Not necessarily. The free ceremonial certificate may be sufficient for a Social Security name change, and a certified county copy is not required simply for the marriage to be valid.

However, another agency or organization may specifically require a certified copy showing that the marriage was officially recorded by Pinal County.

## How many certified copies should we order?

That depends on how many official records you plan to update. Many agencies inspect or temporarily hold the document and then return it, so more than one copy may not be necessary.

Having additional copies can still be convenient if you are updating several records at the same time, mailing documents to different agencies or simply want a backup. Pinal County charges **$35 for each certified abstract copy**.

## How much should we budget?

* **Required marriage license:** $98
* **Ceremony and officiant services:** Free
* **Ceremonial marriage certificate:** Free
* **Optional certified Pinal County copy:** $35 each
* **Total with one certified county copy:** **$133**

Additional certified copies would add **$35 each**.
